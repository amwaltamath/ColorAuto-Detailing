We love working with other great locally owned businesses here in Grand Junction! When Risky Fitness put together their new facility, they reached out to us to help create the perfect space.

## The Project

Risky Fitness needed privacy for their new space, and we delivered the perfect solution: **XPEL Architectural Black Out Film**. This premium film provides ultimate privacy while maintaining a sleek, professional appearance.

## Why XPEL Architectural Black Out Film?

This premium architectural film is ideal for commercial spaces because it offers:

- **Complete Privacy**: Blocks interior views while maintaining light transmission
- **Professional Appearance**: Sleek, modern look that enhances the facility
- **Durability**: Built to withstand high-traffic commercial environments
- **Heat Rejection**: Reduces cooling costs and UV exposure
- **Glare Reduction**: Creates a more comfortable environment for members
- **Easy Maintenance**: Simple to clean and maintain

## What It Does

The Black Out Film creates an ideal gym environment by:

- Protecting member privacy during workouts
- Reducing glare on fitness equipment and mirrors
- Adding a premium, finished look to the space
- Improving energy efficiency
- Blocking harmful UV rays
