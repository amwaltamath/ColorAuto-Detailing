## Why Window Tint Matters in Grand Junction

Western Colorado gets intense sun year-round. Without quality tint, UV rays fade dashboards and seats, heat builds up fast in parked vehicles, and glare makes driving uncomfortable on I-70 and Highway 50.

Professional window tint helps by:

- **Blocking up to 99% of UV rays** — protects your skin and interior
- **Rejecting solar heat** — less AC strain in summer
- **Reducing glare** — safer driving at sunrise and sunset
- **Adding privacy** — especially useful for SUVs, trucks, and commercial spaces

## Colorado Window Tint Laws

Colorado regulates how dark your tint can be. At ColorAuto, every automotive install is compliant with state law.

| Window | Colorado VLT limit |
|--------|-------------------|
| Front side windows | **27% VLT minimum** (must allow more than 27% light in) |
| Back side windows | Any darkness |
| Rear window | Any darkness |
| Windshield | Non-reflective tint allowed on the top 4 inches only |

**VLT** (Visible Light Transmission) measures how much light passes through the film. Lower VLT = darker tint. We help you pick a shade that looks great and stays legal.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is window tint legal in Colorado?**  
Yes — with limits on front side windows (27% VLT minimum). Rear windows can be any darkness.

**How long does tint last?**  
Premium XPEL films last the life of the vehicle when properly installed. Our installs include a manufacturer lifetime warranty.

**Can I tint my windshield?**  
Only a non-reflective visor strip on the top 4 inches is legal in Colorado. We do not tint full windshields on street-legal vehicles.

**Will tint void my factory warranty?**  
No. Window tint is an aftermarket accessory and does not affect your vehicle warranty.
